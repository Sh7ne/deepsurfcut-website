          <summary>Rapid Surf Map: coverage and how to use it</summary>
          <div class="directory-guide-content">
            <p>Rapid Surf Map is a directory of deep-water standing-wave venues by DeepSurfCut. Find venues, compare indoor and outdoor settings, and open sources, operator websites and directions in each listing.</p>
            <h3>What is rapid surfing?</h3><p>Rapid surfing means surfing a standing wave formed naturally or by an artificial system. <a href="https://rapidsurfleague.com/rapid-surfing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Rapid Surf League terminology</a></p>
            <p>This map focuses on commercial deep-water standing waves. It excludes thin-sheet simulators, natural river waves and unverified projects. It is not an exhaustive list of venues.</p>
            <h3>How do I find a venue and booking information?</h3><p>Select a region, then search by city or venue name. Filter for indoor, outdoor or seasonal venues. Open a listing for its address, website, contacts and booking sources, then choose directions. Browsing does not require location access.</p>
            <h3>What do source dates and map pins mean?</h3><p>Each listing retains its check date and source links. A check date does not confirm that a venue is open today, and a season does not confirm an available session. Confirm hours and bookings with the operator. Pins distinguish venue, site and approximate positions; check the entrance before travelling.</p>
            <h3>How can I edit my session footage?</h3><p>For recordings from a fixed camera, see the <a href="/surf-video-editing/#rapid-surfing">rapid surfing video editing guide</a> and <a href="/portrait-follow/">9:16 Portrait Follow tutorial</a>.</p>
