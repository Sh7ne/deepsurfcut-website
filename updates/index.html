iOS & iPadOS

For iPhone and iPad

View on the App Store
  1. 1.3.2 October 12, 2026 Latest release

    Keep every ride together

    • More reliable surfer grouping when people cross paths or tracking switches between people. Short rides are easier to match to the right surfer at lower analysis frame rates.
    • Resuming an interrupted analysis restores more of the tracking and surfer identity state.
    • End-card exports better preserve the selected opening and final frames, frame timing, HDR appearance, and short audio endings.
    • A sound-reactive wave animation, fading ocean audio, and clearer titles bring a more polished finish to your reel.
    • Surfer previews follow the source video's frame cadence more closely. Remaining-time estimates now cover grouping and preview preparation until results are ready.
    • Fewer unnecessary video copies and automatic limits on regenerable caches help manage storage.
    • Unsaved Exports helps recover completed videos that have not yet been saved to Photos.
  2. 1.2.2 October 3, 2026

    Choose your scan precision

    • Choose Low, Medium, or High scan precision in Settings, with Medium as the default. Resumed scans keep their original precision.
    • Optional end cards use a four-second blurred background from the final exported frame, with a larger app icon and a clearer title.
  3. 1.2.0 October 2, 2026

    Smoother follows. More reliable edits.

    • Smoother Portrait Follow exports, clearer crops at the source resolution, and improved audio endings.
    • More reliable clip merging, splitting, project reopening, and export order.
    • Updated on-device surfer and surfboard detection, with clearer guidance for adjusting the surf area and using the timeline.
    • Improved end-card compatibility, with a one-second final logo hold.
    • Refined Quick Start layouts and a new hint for collapsing surfer cards.
  4. 1.0.1 September 25, 2026

    Portrait Follow and more editing control

    • Portrait Follow creates 9:16 videos that follow your selected surfer, with smoother framing through brief tracking gaps.
    • More reliable tracking and grouping when surfers overlap, turn, move farther from the camera, or cross the pool boundaries.
    • Better camera-angle support: Improved pool-area detection for tilted or non-level camera views.
    • Create surfer tracks manually, move clips to the right track, and regenerate surfer thumbnails. Project saving, reopening, and timeline editing are more reliable.
    • Export the final 1-10 seconds of a clip, adjust footage before and after each ride, and follow progress per surfer. Cancel exports while keeping completed files.
    • Add an optional branded outro matched to landscape or portrait output, with better support for trimmed clips and different frame rates.
    • Change the app's language directly in Settings. DeepSurfCut Pro works across iPhone, iPad, and Mac with the same Apple Account.
    • Clearer long-video analysis, slow-processing options, and error messages. Feedback does not automatically attach videos or images.
    • Improved background analysis, progress updates, and interrupted-scan recovery, subject to system availability.
    • Check available storage before large imports, recover completed exports if saving to Photos fails, and edit on iPad in portrait, landscape, or resized windows.
  5. 0.9.9 August 6, 2026

    Faster timeline navigation

    • Improved surfer tracking, timeline navigation, and export reliability. Quickly center the timeline on the yellow playhead and display detected analysis areas.
    • Press and hold a segment to export it, view export progress, or export only the final 10 seconds of selected clips. Supported exports can continue in the background.
