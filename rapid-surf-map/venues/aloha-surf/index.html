About the venue

Complexe Aloha’s 10 m wide FlowSurf standing wave can be adjusted to a height of up to 1.5 m. Standard and private sessions are available.

Opening and booking

Open year-round. A standard booking includes 45 minutes of shared wave time, 15 minutes of instruction, a board and a wetsuit.

Opening information is not live. Check session availability before you travel.
