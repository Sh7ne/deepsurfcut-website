DeepSurfCut support

Capture the whole wave.
Keep every ride clear.

DeepSurfCut is built for session-length, fixed-camera recordings at deep-water standing-wave venues. A clean, consistent view gives the first pass better material to work with.

Lock off one wide shot

Mount the camera so the entry, wave, and fall zone stay in the same place for the full session. Avoid handheld follow shots and frequent reframing.

Keep the full riding zone clear

Frame the complete wave without cropping the rider's entry or fall. Avoid panning, digital zoom, and obstructions between the camera and the riding zone.

Give the camera clean light

DeepSurfCut is designed around high-quality, fixed-camera footage. Strong backlight, low light, glare, and heavy compression can make riders harder to follow.

Review the first pass

The AI pass is editable by design. Check ride boundaries and rider groups, then trim, move, merge, or remove clips before the final handoff.

Supported workflow

  • Import a full MOV or MP4 session recording.
  • Analyze fixed-camera deep-water standing-wave footage.
  • Review rider cards and rides on a multitrack timeline.
  • Save and reopen DeepSurfCut projects.
  • Export selected riders as separate clips or complete reels.

Platforms

DeepSurfCut is available on the App Store for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. See the App Store product page for current operating system and device compatibility.

Contact

For product questions or support, email support@deepsurf.app. When reporting a problem, include the device model, operating system version, video format, and the step where the issue occurred. Do not attach private source footage unless requested.
