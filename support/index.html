DeepSurfCut support

Better footage in.
Better rides out.

DeepSurfCut is built for fixed-camera standing-wave surfing recordings. These basics help the first pass work at its best.

Record from a fixed position

Mount the camera so the riding, entry, and exit areas stay in the same regions throughout the session. Avoid panning, handheld follow shots, and frequent reframing.

Keep the full riding zone visible

Frame the complete wave and avoid cutting off the entry or fall zone. Clear separation between the rider and people waiting nearby improves detection.

Use clear, well-lit footage

DeepSurfCut is designed around high-quality iPhone Pro footage. Strong backlight, darkness, heavy digital zoom, and severe obstruction can reduce result quality.

Review before exporting

AI results are intentionally editable. Check clip boundaries and surfer groups, then trim, move, merge, or remove clips before creating the final export.

Supported workflow

  • Import MOV and MP4 session recordings.
  • Analyze fixed-camera standing-wave surfing footage.
  • Review surfer cards and multitrack timeline results.
  • Save and reopen DeepSurfCut projects.
  • Export selected surfers as separate clips or combined reels.

Platforms

DeepSurfCut is being developed for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Final operating system and device compatibility will be listed on the App Store product page at release.

Contact

For product questions, launch updates, or support, email support@deepsurf.app. When reporting a problem, include the device model, operating system version, video format, and the step where the issue occurred. Do not attach private source footage unless requested.
