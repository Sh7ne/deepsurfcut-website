开始使用 / Getting started

  1. 从照片或文件导入横屏冲浪视频。
    Import a landscape surfing video from Photos or Files.
  2. 已剪辑的合集选择合集模式；固定机位的原片选择连续拍摄模式。
    Choose montage mode for edited clips or continuous mode for a fixed-camera recording.
  3. 开始分析，并保持 App 在前台。
    Start analysis and keep the app in the foreground.
  4. 检查每个镜头的主体和跟随效果，再导出 9:16 视频。
    Review the subject and crop in each shot, then export a 9:16 video.

跟随不准确 / Tracking looks wrong

多人重叠、主体太小、遮挡或频繁转场可能影响识别。请优先使用清晰、稳定的横屏素材，并在分析后逐镜头检查主体选择。

Small subjects, occlusion, overlapping people and rapid transitions can affect tracking. Use clear footage and review subject selection shot by shot.

导出或保存失败 / Export or save failed

检查设备剩余空间。保存到照片需要允许添加照片的权限；也可以使用系统分享保存到文件。长视频处理时请保持前台运行。

Check available storage. Saving to Photos requires add-only photo permission. You can also use the system share sheet to save to Files.
